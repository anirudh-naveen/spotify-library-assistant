Spotassist reads a playlist, looks up each artist's genre tags on Last.fm, and adds each song to the genre playlists you set up. You can also remove songs that don't fit a playlist. Log in with Spotify to start.

Clean up a playlist

Pick one of your playlists and what it should be. Spotassist lists the songs whose artists match none of those genres' keywords on Last.fm. You choose which to remove, and nothing changes until you confirm.

Genres

A song joins a genre when one of its artist's Last.fm tags is in that genre's keywords. Each genre goes to the playlist you pick: one you own, or a new private playlist with any name. Several genres can share a playlist, and the same song is never added twice.
